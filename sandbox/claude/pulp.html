THOUSAND FACES · issue 137p. X

On the beauty of a misprint

A reader writes to complain that our colours keep wandering — the red is off the face, the blue is off the sky. We reply, in all seriousness: three cents a copy, and you still expect a life that lines up?

Keep the misprints. In fifty years they are artefacts; until then they are our signature.

— the editor, while the type is still warm

THOUSAND FACES · issue 137p. XII

The Foggy Gallows · chapter nine, a night of rain

Rain hammered the tin roof over the pier like a composing room working late. She folded away the green raincoat and left one line behind: “One man short on the gallows means one more name on the list, and it is yours.”

The lamp went out. On the table the pocket watch had stopped at a quarter to twelve.

To be continued next issue — if next issue gets printed.
