Tabs · the seventh firing in four leaves

7thThrew eleven pieces, kept eight; the rest back to slurry.

9thTrimmed the feet, stamped the “plain” mark underneath.

11thShelved to dry in shade, under the shed, out of the wind.

13thTwo dips of persimmon red; foot ring wiped clean.

14thChecked for pinholes under the lamp; filled three, logged them.

16thLit before dawn; a low fire to warm the kiln, the smoke rising straight.

17thTurned to reduction at dusk; through the door gap the flame reads orange going white.

20thMorning. Kiln cool, door opened. Nine of eleven came good.
